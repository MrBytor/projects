Class A 90 minutes

Numbers and counting

Learning objectives ↓

Build the mathematical toolkit for statistics: use set notation and Venn diagrams, select counting methods, classify numbers and calculate accurately using PEMDAS.

What this class covers

  • Course introduction, evidence and sampling through the Mystery Bag demonstration.
  • Set membership, cardinality, empty and universal sets; union, intersection and complement; two-set Venn diagrams.
  • Basic counting principle, factorials, permutations and combinations; explain whether roles or order matter.
  • Natural, whole, integer, rational, irrational and real numbers.
  • Order of operations with powers, roots and nested brackets; diagnose equal-priority left-to-right errors.

Class B 90 minutes

Calculations in context

Learning objectives ↓

Solve linear and simple quadratic equations, convert units, and use percentages and ratios to make and justify numerical decisions.

What this class covers

  • Variables, signed coefficients, constants and like terms; expressions versus equations; simplify, substitute and expand.
  • Solve linear equations by balancing both sides; verify by substitution.
  • Recognise quadratics and solve by factorisation or the quadratic formula.
  • Metric and imperial unit conversions, conversion factors and interpreting capacity limits.
  • Percentages of a whole, reverse percentages, discounts and percentage change using the starting value.
  • Simplify and scale ratios, split totals, distinguish part-to-part from part-to-whole, and recover a missing total.

Class C 90 minutes

Applied problem solving

Learning objectives ↓

Apply the Week 1 toolkit to a connected product case, from customer evidence and product choices to packaging, material quantities and sales revenue.

What this class covers

  • GreenSip customer-feature survey: organise overlapping responses in a Venn diagram and distinguish the surveyed group from wider claims.
  • Count product combinations; choose permutations or combinations for testers and different roles.
  • Form and check a linear equation for bottle mass; convert batch mass and assess a planning limit.
  • Model rectangular packaging with a quadratic equation and choose physically valid dimensions.
  • Work backwards from production loss, divide materials in a ratio, calculate discounted prices and compare revenue with a target.
  • Complete an independent EcoCup transfer task and identify assumptions that still need evidence.
