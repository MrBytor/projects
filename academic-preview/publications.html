Outsiders within: Exploring integration strategies of international faculty in Chinese higher education institutions

Ding, J., Yang, L., & Mlynarski, J.

Higher Education · Online publication

PublisherDOI
Abstract summary

This study examines how international faculty navigate their position within Chinese higher education. It explores the strategies used to participate in institutional life while maintaining an outsider perspective, and considers what this means for more inclusive universities.

Vocabulary Size Assessment as a Predictor of Plagiarism

Dodigovic, M., Mlynarski, J., & Wei, R.

In Current Issues in Language Evaluation, Assessment and Testing: Research and Practice · pp. 222–235

Publication details
Abstract summary

The chapter examines vocabulary knowledge and plagiarism in the writing of more than 200 learners of English as a second language. It reports a strong negative relationship between vocabulary size and plagiarism rate, highlighting vocabulary teaching as one possible way to support academic integrity.
