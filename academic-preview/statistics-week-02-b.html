Week 02 · Class B90 minutesFoundation · Business & Science

Sampling and collecting data

What this class covers

  • Proportional stratified allocation, random selection within each group and fixed-total rounding; equal versus proportional group samples.
  • Cluster sampling of whole classes; comparison with stratified and simple random procedures.
  • Sampling variation versus bias; coverage/selection issues, nonresponse, leading questions and systematic measurement error.
  • Response rates versus support among respondents; optional logical bounds for unknown responses.
  • Accuracy versus precision using repeated-sample plots; why sample size does not repair biased procedures.
  • Select a defensible sampling method using cost, access and statistical limitations; evaluate and rewrite survey headlines.

Learning objectives

  • Allocate a stratified sample and explain exactly how participants will be selected.
  • Distinguish stratified, cluster and simple random sampling from their procedures.
  • Diagnose selection, nonresponse, response and measurement problems and propose precise repairs.
  • Calculate response and respondent-support percentages using the correct denominators.
  • Distinguish accuracy from precision and defend a sampling choice with its main limitation.
  • Assess whether a survey claim is supported by the reported evidence.
