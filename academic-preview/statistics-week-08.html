Sampling distributions

  2. 8A.2Calculate and interpret the standard error of a sample mean.
  3. 8A.3Explain how the Central Limit Theorem supports an approximate normal model for sample means.

Confidence intervals

Back to class ↑
  1. 8B.1Distinguish a point estimate from a confidence interval.
  2. 8B.2Select a suitable z- or t-based interval for a population mean.
  3. 8B.3Calculate a confidence interval and interpret its confidence level and margin of error.
