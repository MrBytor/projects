Lecturer · School of Business Administration

Jacob Mlynarski is a lecturer and project coordinator at Xi’an Eurasia University. His professional experience spans approximately 17 years in education in China, encompassing university teaching, curriculum development and academic leadership.

His teaching areas include Statistics, Corporate Finance, Project Management, management, human resource management and organisational behaviour. He has also taught English for Academic Purposes and research methods within international university preparation programmes.

His academic leadership experience includes Academic Principal and NCUK programme and centre leadership roles. These responsibilities have included academic operations, programme development, assessment, staff development and student progression to overseas universities.

Research and academic development. His published work examines international faculty integration in Chinese universities, internationalisation in private higher education, brand equity and vocabulary assessment in relation to plagiarism. His academic development work includes curriculum standards, quality assurance and EFMD EPAS accreditation planning.
