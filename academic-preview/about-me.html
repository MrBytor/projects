Lecturer · School of Business Administration

I am a lecturer and project coordinator at Xi’an Eurasia University. I have approximately 17 years of experience in education in China, spanning university teaching, curriculum development and academic leadership.

I teach Statistics, Corporate Finance, Project Management, management, human resource management and organisational behaviour. I have also taught English for Academic Purposes and research methods within international university preparation programmes.

My academic leadership experience includes serving as an Academic Principal and leading NCUK programmes and centres. My responsibilities have included academic operations, programme development, assessment, staff development and supporting students’ progression to overseas universities.

Research and academic development. My published work examines international faculty integration in Chinese universities, internationalisation in private higher education, brand equity and vocabulary assessment in relation to plagiarism. My academic development work includes curriculum standards, quality assurance and EFMD EPAS accreditation planning.
