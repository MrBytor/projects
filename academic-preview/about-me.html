About Me

Teaching, research and academic leadership in an international setting.

University lecturer · Xi’an, China

Jacob Mlynarski

I’m a university lecturer at Xi’an Eurasia University, with around 17 years of experience in education in China. My work connects business education, academic skills and programme leadership.

I teach Statistics, Corporate Finance and Project Management. I have also taught management, human resource management, organisational behaviour, English for Academic Purposes and research methods.

Alongside teaching, I have worked on curriculum development, academic quality assurance and international programme coordination. My leadership experience includes Academic Principal and NCUK centre roles, supporting teachers and helping students progress to university study.

How I teach

Students need a clear route from seeing a new idea to using it independently. I introduce each concept through a worked example, guide students through practice, then ask them to apply the method themselves.

  1. 01See a worked example
  2. 02Practise together
  3. 03Try it independently

I use practical cases, clear explanations and constructive feedback to help students understand both the calculation and what its result means.
