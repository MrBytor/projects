Course topics

01

Working with numbers

Ratios, percentages, unit conversions, algebra, factorials, permutations and combinations.

Populations and samples, parameters and statistics, types of data and sampling methods.

03

Frequency tables, mean, median, mode, quartiles, percentiles, range and interquartile range.

Deviations, population and sample variance, standard deviation, coefficient of variation and distribution shape.

05

Presenting data in Excel

Frequency tables, histograms, frequency polygons, cumulative frequencies and ogives.

06

Understanding probability

Sets and Venn diagrams, probability rules and probability trees, supported by guided practice.
