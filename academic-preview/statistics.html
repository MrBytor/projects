About Course

Statistics helps us turn data into evidence. In this 12-week foundation course, you will learn how to collect reliable data, summarise a dataset, present results clearly and use probability to understand uncertainty.

We begin with the calculations needed for statistics, then move through sampling, descriptive measures, charts and Excel. Later topics introduce probability distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis tests and regression, using examples from business and science.

Each concept follows a clear sequence: a worked demonstration, guided practice and an independent task. You will practise calculations, interpret what the answers mean and explain your conclusions in plain English.

What Will You Learn?

  • Choose sampling methods and recognise bias.
  • Describe the centre, spread and shape of data.
  • Build and interpret statistical charts in Excel.
  • Calculate probabilities and use distribution models.
  • Estimate population values and test claims.
  • Interpret correlation and regression results.
