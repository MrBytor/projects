About Course

Corporate Finance introduces the financial logic behind business decisions. In this 16-week course, you will explore how companies obtain funds, invest resources, manage daily operations and distribute profits, and how these choices affect company value.

We begin with financial statements and analysis, then develop the tools for valuing cash flows, understanding risk and evaluating investments. Later modules connect financing and capital structure with working capital and profit distribution. An integrated case and group presentation bring these decisions together before the final review.

Designed for Human Resource Management students, the course connects financial evidence with budgets, staffing investments, training proposals and operational trade-offs. Worked examples, guided calculations and group cases help you explain what the numbers mean and justify a recommendation.

What Will You Learn?

  • Explain the four financial decisions and interpret financial statements and ratios.
  • Apply time value of money and risk–return tools, including beta and CAPM.
  • Estimate project cash flows and evaluate investments using NPV, IRR and payback.
  • Compare financing sources, cost of capital and capital structure choices.
  • Analyse working capital, dividends, retained earnings and share repurchases.
  • Use financial evidence to defend a responsible business recommendation.

Course Content

5 modules · 16 classes

One 90-minute class each week, equivalent to two academic hours. The course comprises 32 academic hours across five modules and a final review.

Module 1 · Financial Foundations and Understanding Company Value3 classes
01

Class 01 · Introduction to Corporate Finance and Financial Decision Framework

Explore the purpose of corporate finance, distinguish the four financial decisions and explain how cash flow, time and risk affect company value.

90 min
02

Class 02 · Financial Statements: Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flow Statement

Read the balance sheet, income statement and cash flow statement, explain how they connect and distinguish accounting profit from cash flow.

90 min
03

Class 03 · Financial Analysis: Profitability, Liquidity, Efficiency, and Solvency

Calculate and interpret profitability, liquidity, efficiency and solvency ratios to assess financial performance and identify management priorities.

90 min
Module 2 · Value Creation and Investment Decisions4 classes
04

Class 04 · Time Value of Money: Compounding, Present Value, Future Value, and Annuities

Use cash-flow timelines, compounding and discounting to calculate present value, future value and annuities.

90 min
05

Class 05 · Risk and Return: Standard Deviation, Beta, CAPM, and Alpha Concept

Calculate expected return and standard deviation, explain diversification and interpret beta, CAPM required returns and alpha.

90 min
06

Class 06 · Investment Decisions: Capital Budgeting and Relevant Cash Flows

Identify incremental project cash flows, exclude sunk costs and account for opportunity costs, working capital and terminal cash flows.

90 min
07

Class 07 · Investment Evaluation: NPV, IRR, Payback, Sensitivity, and Scenario Analysis

Evaluate investments using payback, NPV and IRR, then test the recommendation through sensitivity and scenario analysis.

90 min
Module 3 · Financing Decisions and Capital Structure3 classes
08

Class 08 · Sources of Financing: Internal Funds, Debt, Equity, Bonds, Shares, and IPOs

Compare internal funds, debt and equity, including bonds, shares and IPOs, and weigh their effects on control, flexibility and risk.

90 min
09

Class 09 · Cost of Capital: Cost of Debt, Cost of Equity, CAPM, and WACC

Estimate the cost of debt and equity, calculate WACC and explain when it is suitable as a discount rate.

90 min
10

Class 10 · Capital Structure: Debt, Equity, Leverage, Financial Risk, and Optimal Mix

Assess debt–equity choices, leverage and financial distress to recommend a capital structure that balances risk and flexibility.

90 min
Module 4 · Operational Financial Management3 classes
11

Class 11 · Working Capital Management and the Cash Conversion Cycle

Analyse current assets and liabilities, calculate the cash conversion cycle and evaluate ways to improve liquidity.

90 min
12

Class 12 · Daily Financial Management: Cash, Inventory, Receivables, and EOQ Concept

Evaluate cash, inventory and receivables decisions, including credit policy and the economic order quantity trade-off.

90 min
13

Class 13 · Profit Distribution: Dividend Policy, Retained Earnings, and Share Repurchases

Compare retained earnings, dividends and share repurchases, considering investment needs, investor expectations and stakeholder effects.

90 min
Module 5 · Integrated Corporate Finance Decisions and Case Applications2 classes
14

Class 14 · Integrated Case Analysis: Investment, Financing, Operations, and Value Creation

Bring investment, financing and operating decisions together in a company case and defend an evidence-based recommendation.

90 min
15

Class 15 · Corporate Finance Case Presentation and Summary

Present a financial recommendation, defend assumptions and calculations, and use questions and peer feedback to improve the analysis.

90 min
Course Review and Exam Preparation1 class
16

Class 16 · Course Review and Exam Preparation

Review key concepts and formulas, practise selecting calculation methods and identify personal priorities for exam preparation.

90 min
