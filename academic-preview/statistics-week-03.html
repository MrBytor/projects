Descriptive statistics

Class A 90 minutes

Centre, spread and distribution shape

Learning objectives ↓

Mean, median and mode; range, quartiles using n + 1, IQR and five-number summaries; interpret skewness and the effect of extreme values.

What this class covers

  • Mean, median and mode; sample and population mean notation
  • Effect of correcting, deleting or replacing an extreme observation
  • Range and why equal ranges can conceal different spread
  • Quartile positions using (n+1)p with linear interpolation; IQR and five-number summary
  • Positive, negative and symmetric distribution shapes; choosing suitable summaries

Class B 90 minutes

Understanding variation

Learning objectives ↓

Deviations, population and sample variance, standard deviation, coefficient of variation and kurtosis; calculate and interpret absolute and relative spread.

What this class covers

  • Signed deviations and why deviations from the mean sum to zero
  • Squared deviations, population variance and standard deviation
  • Target population versus sample; sample variance using n−1 and sample standard deviation
  • Absolute versus relative variation; coefficient of variation
  • Kurtosis and the influence of extreme values; ordinary kurtosis benchmark of 3
  • Optional backwards problem finding missing values from the mean and sample SD

Class C 90 minutes

Paper supplier workshop

Learning objectives ↓

Compare two suppliers using delivery-time averages, IQR, sample SD and CV; justify decisions for routine orders and changing deadlines. Formative Assessment 1 follows the workshop.

What this class covers

  • Revision of sample/population notation, centre, spread, quartiles, deviation, sample SD and CV
  • Two suppliers with 30 simulated delivery times each
  • Mean, median, range, (n+1)p quartiles, IQR, sample variance, SD and CV
  • Evidence-based recommendation for routine A4-paper orders
  • On-time counts and percentages at 48-hour and 54-hour deadlines
  • Trade-offs, remaining delivery risk and why minimum SD alone does not determine the best supplier
