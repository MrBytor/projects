Statistics with Project Skills

Paper supplier workshop

Week 03 · Class C90 minutesFoundation · Business & Science

Descriptive statistics

What this class covers

  • Revision of sample/population notation, centre, spread, quartiles, deviation, sample SD and CV
  • Two suppliers with 30 simulated delivery times each
  • Mean, median, range, (n+1)p quartiles, IQR, sample variance, SD and CV
  • Evidence-based recommendation for routine A4-paper orders
  • On-time counts and percentages at 48-hour and 54-hour deadlines
  • Trade-offs, remaining delivery risk and why minimum SD alone does not determine the best supplier

Learning objectives

  • Analyse both suppliers using measures of typical delivery time and variation.
  • Write a recommendation supported by numerical comparisons and business meaning.
  • Reassess the decision when the delivery deadline changes.
  • Distinguish historical on-time percentages from a guarantee for the next delivery.
