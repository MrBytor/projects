Statistics with Project Skills

Survey workshop

Week 02 · Class C90 minutesFoundation · Business & Science

Sampling and collecting data

What this class covers

  • Distinguish a reduction in portion size from a reduction in measured waste using a two-day pilot.
  • Calculate response rate, support among respondents and logical lower/upper support bounds among invitees.
  • Identify club-only coverage, leading wording and a miscalibrated scale; write a neutral survey question with balanced options.
  • Compare individual simple random selection with pair-based selection; reconstruct systematic sampling sequences.
  • Allocate proportional programme samples, interpret a realised cluster imbalance and diagnose programme-specific nonresponse.
  • Classify canteen variables, retain valid zeros, evaluate survey headlines and write a concise corrected report.
  • Seminar cases on changing population definitions, unequal sampling fractions, daytime-only contact, list periodicity and methods matched to study needs.

Learning objectives

  • Audit numerical claims and distinguish descriptive comparisons from causal evidence.
  • Use correct denominators and missing-response bounds when interpreting survey support.
  • Repair biased or unclear selection, question wording and measurement procedures.
  • Justify a sampling design and examine what happens between invitations and replies.
  • Classify recorded variables and write a short report limited to what the evidence supports.
