Statistics with Project Skills

Calculations in context

Week 01 · Class B90 minutesFoundation · Business & Science

Mathematical foundations

What this class covers

  • Variables, signed coefficients, constants and like terms; expressions versus equations; simplify, substitute and expand.
  • Solve linear equations by balancing both sides; verify by substitution.
  • Recognise quadratics and solve by factorisation or the quadratic formula.
  • Metric and imperial unit conversions, conversion factors and interpreting capacity limits.
  • Percentages of a whole, reverse percentages, discounts and percentage change using the starting value.
  • Simplify and scale ratios, split totals, distinguish part-to-part from part-to-whole, and recover a missing total.

Learning objectives

  • Simplify algebraic expressions and solve linear equations.
  • Recognise and solve simple quadratic equations.
  • Convert measurement units with appropriate units and rounding.
  • Select the correct percentage or ratio method and interpret the result in context.
