Class A 90 minutes

Organising and summarising data

Learning objectives ↓

Frequency and percentage tables, bar and pie charts, class intervals and midpoints, estimated grouped means, and stem-and-leaf plots.

What this class covers

  • Categorical frequencies, relative frequencies and percentages
  • Vertical and horizontal bar charts; pie-chart sector angles and construction
  • Nonoverlapping lower-inclusive upper-exclusive class intervals and class midpoints
  • Grouped frequency tables, modal class and midpoint-based estimated mean
  • Stem-and-leaf plots for integer and decimal data, including repeats and empty stems
  • Independent selection of class intervals and explanation of information lost through grouping

Class B 90 minutes

Distributions and relationships

Learning objectives ↓

Histograms, frequency polygons, cumulative tables and ogives; estimate medians, plot relationships, use a line of best fit and compare boxplots.

What this class covers

  • Equal-width histograms from raw data and the effect of changing interval width
  • Frequency polygons and relative-frequency comparisons
  • Cumulative frequency and cumulative percentage tables
  • Ogives at upper class boundaries; threshold counts and grouped median interpolation
  • Scatterplots, explanatory and response variables, association and causation
  • Straight lines of best fit, predictions and extrapolation limits
  • Five-number summaries and boxplots using (n+1)p quartiles

Class C 90 minutes

Campus café Excel practical

Learning objectives ↓

Use café data to build frequency tables, a bar chart, histogram and scatterplot in Excel; interpret the evidence and recommend how to reduce food waste.

What this class covers

  • Revision and classification of meal ID, meal type, satisfaction, item count, portion and leftover weights
  • Matched Demo (20 records) and Data (300 records) worksheet layouts
  • COUNTA, filtering distinct category labels, COUNTIF and fixed references
  • Category frequencies, shares and a bar chart
  • COUNTIFS for lower-inclusive upper-exclusive intervals, histogram and threshold counts
  • Scatterplot of portion and leftover weights; paired observations and association limits
  • Recommendation to reduce food waste, supported by figures, a chart and a satisfaction check
  • Optional cumulative-percentage graph outside the 90-minute core
