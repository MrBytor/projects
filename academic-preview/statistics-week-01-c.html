Statistics with Project Skills

Applied problem solving

Week 01 · Class C90 minutesFoundation · Business & Science

Mathematical foundations

What this class covers

  • GreenSip customer-feature survey: organise overlapping responses in a Venn diagram and distinguish the surveyed group from wider claims.
  • Count product combinations; choose permutations or combinations for testers and different roles.
  • Form and check a linear equation for bottle mass; convert batch mass and assess a planning limit.
  • Model rectangular packaging with a quadratic equation and choose physically valid dimensions.
  • Work backwards from production loss, divide materials in a ratio, calculate discounted prices and compare revenue with a target.
  • Complete an independent EcoCup transfer task and identify assumptions that still need evidence.

Learning objectives

  • Choose and justify mathematical methods across a connected case.
  • Organise overlapping survey evidence and count product or team possibilities.
  • Solve contextual linear and quadratic equations and check units and dimensions.
  • Calculate quantities, losses, ratios, discounts and revenue.
  • Separate a correct calculation from a conclusion the evidence actually supports.
